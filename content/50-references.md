### 17. References & Further Reading

> **STUB.** Add entries below using the strict format shown. Store links and your own one-line
> note — never paste chapters, transcripts, or article text into this repo. Copying someone
> else's writing into a published app is a copyright problem and bloats the offline bundle.

Each entry uses this shape:

#### Example resource title
- id: `ref-example-slug`
- type: `book` | `video` | `article` | `channel` | `paper`
- author: Name of author or channel
- url: https://example.com/the-actual-link
- note: One line, in your own words, on what this covers and when it is worth reading.

**Rules**

- `id` must start with `ref-` and be unique across the whole repo.
- `note` is your summary, not the source's own description.
- Omit `url` for books; include publisher and year in `author` instead.
- Anything you cannot link to publicly does not belong in this file.
