#### Stage 1: Footwork foundations
- id: `stage-footwork-foundations`
- techniques: `inside-edge, outside-edge, smear, back-step, rock-over`
- drills: `silent-feet, glue-feet, sniper`
- summary: Precise, quiet feet and basic weight-shifting come before anything else — most of a beginner's unclaimed progress lives here.

#### Stage 2: Body position & balance
- id: `stage-body-position-balance`
- techniques: `outside-flag, back-flag, perching, frogging, hand-match, foot-match`
- drills: `hip-kiss, straight-arms-only, no-barn-door`
- summary: Get the hips to the wall and the arms straight before adding power — flags and matching keep balance under control.

#### Stage 3: Grip fundamentals
- id: `stage-grip-fundamentals`
- techniques: `open-hand-three-finger-drag, open-crimp, half-crimp, pinch, palm-down-palming`
- drills: `grip-awareness`
- summary: Build the safest, most trainable grip positions first — the stronger, riskier grips can wait.

#### Stage 4: Reading holds & positions
- id: `stage-reading-holds-positions`
- techniques: `side-pull, lay-back, gaston, undercling, heel-hook, toe-hook, mantle`
- drills: `flag-hunt, one-touch`
- summary: Learn to read which direction a hold actually pulls, and the positions that let you use it.

#### Stage 5: Power & dynamic movement
- id: `stage-power-dynamic-movement`
- techniques: `dead-point, dyno, hip-swing, drop-knee`
- drills: `deadpoint-practice, static-then-dynamic`
- summary: Add momentum and commitment once the static fundamentals are solid — this is also where injury risk starts climbing, so go gradually.

#### Stage 6: Competition & specialty moves
- id: `stage-competition-specialty-moves`
- techniques: `inside-figure-4, outside-figure-4, one-arm-dyno, double-clutch, rose-move`
- drills: `two-solutions`
- summary: Rare, high-skill moves worth knowing by name — not a training priority, but useful once everything above is comfortable.
