A warm-up has three jobs: raise tissue temperature, prepare the specific joints climbing loads hardest, and rehearse movement before intensity. Skipping any of the three is how sessions start badly.

**Suggested structure to fill in**

- General: 5 minutes raising heart rate off the wall.
- Joint preparation: wrists, fingers, forearms, elbows, shoulders, hips.
- Movement rehearsal: easy climbing well below limit, gradually steepening.
- Ramp: progressively harder problems until you reach working grade.
